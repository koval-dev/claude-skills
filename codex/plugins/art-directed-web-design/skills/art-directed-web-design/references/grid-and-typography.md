# Grid and Typography

## Grid

- Establish common left and right anchors before styling individual sections.
- Choose column counts to fit the content; 6, 8, and 12 are useful starting points for editorial pages.
- Give primary content more space than supporting content; empty columns can provide emphasis.
- Tie selective offsets and overlap to grid anchors and keep them robust across widths.
- Separate full-bleed backgrounds from full-width readable content.
- Use nested grids when they preserve parent anchors and avoid unrelated container widths.

## Section rhythm

- Follow dense content with breathing room when the narrative allows it.
- Mark meaningful transitions with scale, measure, placement, or a restrained colour field.
- Repeat a small number of compositional relationships; vary them according to content.
- Keep labels and captions purposeful and subordinate rather than attaching an eyebrow to every heading.

## Typography

- Aim for roughly 55–75 characters per line for sustained body copy and narrower measures for emphasis.
- Use scale contrast and a small set of heading relationships drawn from existing tokens.
- Let spacing, placement, and measure contribute to hierarchy.
- Keep deliberate line breaks sensible at narrower widths.
- Use uppercase sparingly and inspect awkward single-word lines and rag shape.

## Mobile transformation

- Preserve the dominant message while adapting proportions and media placement.
- Keep accessible DOM order logical and key labels close to their content.
- Remove fragile decorative overlap and retain alignment through consistent gutters.
- Use scale, dividers, and varied density to distinguish the roles of stacked content.
